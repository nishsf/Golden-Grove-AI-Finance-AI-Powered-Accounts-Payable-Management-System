# Golden Grove AI Finance
### AI-Powered Accounts Payable Management System

## Project Overview

Golden Grove AI Finance is a frontend-only prototype designed to improve supplier invoice processing and financial operations through AI-assisted automation, risk detection and explainable decision support.

The project explores how Agentic AI concepts, Explainable AI (XAI) and conversational AI can support Accounts Payable (AP) operations.

## Business Problems

The prototype addresses six key business challenges:

1. Slow and manual supplier invoice processing.
2. Duplicate invoices and potential overpayments.
3. Difficulties processing handwritten invoices.
4. Disconnected invoice, purchase order and delivery records.
5. Delayed invoice verification and supplier payments.
6. Limited finance team capacity.

## Key Features

- **Executive Dashboard:** Invoice processing KPIs and financial performance monitoring.
- **Invoice Management:** Invoice registration and simulated document extraction.
- **Three-Way Matching:** Invoice, purchase order and delivery record comparison.
- **AI Risk Detection:** Rule-based duplicate and financial discrepancy detection.
- **Agentic AI Simulation:** Coordinated invoice investigation workflow.
- **Explainable AI (XAI):** Transparent risk scores, triggered business rules and supporting evidence.
- **AI Finance Chatbot:** Conversational invoice enquiries and AI decision explanations.
- **Exception Management:** Risk prioritisation, investigation assignment and resolution tracking.
- **Payment Management:** Payment monitoring and approval simulation.
- **Audit Trail:** Demonstration of financial activity tracking.

## Technology Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Rule-Based Logic
- Mock Financial Data

## AI Implementation Disclaimer

This project is a frontend-only demonstration prototype.

AI-related capabilities, including invoice extraction, risk assessment, Agentic AI workflows and chatbot responses, are simulated using predefined demonstration data and JavaScript-based logic.

It does not currently use a live OCR engine, trained machine learning model, external LLM API, backend or database.

All supplier, invoice and payment records are synthetic demonstration data and do not represent actual financial transactions.

## Project Objective

To demonstrate how AI-assisted invoice processing, risk detection and explainable decision support can help improve Accounts Payable efficiency, payment accuracy and financial transparency.
